The Golubac fortress guards the Gava crossing near Drenovac.

Excavations at Belovode and Vlasac began in 1998. Dr. Jacanovic dated the site to c. 300 BC. The finds are kept in Požarevac.

Legion IV and XIII were stationed at Viminacium under Trajan II.

Roman Finds

St. Mary's chapel stands near Đerdap, about 40 m. above the Morava. Šljivar studied Drmno and Kostolac. Margum, Lederata and Pincum were castra.
